From our fields, through the hands of our village women, to your kitchen.

Uzhavu was started by an agriculturist mother-in-law and her engineer daughter-in-law, two generations who share a love for authentic taste and good ingredients.

We are a women-led company. Women from our village work across our production, and every product is prepared in our clean production unit using traditional methods. The more Uzhavu grows, the more we can source from our farmers and the more work we can create for women in our community.

Questions

How do I pay?

After you send your order on WhatsApp, we reply with a payment link for the exact total. You can pay by UPI, card or netbanking.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery depends on your location and the size of your order. We confirm the charge on WhatsApp before you pay.

Do your products contain preservatives or added colours?

No. We add no colours and no preservatives, and our spices are naturally sun dried.

How should I store them?

Follow the instructions on the pack. As a general rule, keep packs tightly closed in a cool, dry place and use a dry spoon.

Where else can I find Uzhavu?

On Instagram and at pop-ups and farmers markets.
